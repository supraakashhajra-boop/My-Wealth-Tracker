Important Accounting Notes

Realized P&L is calculated using FIFO lots and transaction charges. Open-lot cost includes buy-side charges. Unrealized P&L uses the last manually updated price. Corporate actions, dividends, taxes and broker statement reconciliation are not yet automated.
